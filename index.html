Merkblatt für Angler
How to fish · Comment pêcher · Come pescare
  1. Find water. Fish hold in seams beside fast current, behind boulders, in deep pools and slack water near banks. Drive with WSAD, drop anchor with Q. The current is strong — upstream needs throttle.
  2. Pick a lure (1–4). Spinners and minnows trigger trout, huchen and pike; spoons tempt char and perch; the worm & float drifts naturally for grayling, whitefish, barbel and chub.
  3. Cast. Press F for fishing mode. Hold left mouse to charge, release in the marked zone for a long cast. Press Space mid-flight to stop the line.
  4. Retrieve. Hold left mouse to reel. Watch the float or feel the rod tip twitch.
  5. Strike! When a fish bites, click right mouse quickly to set the hook.
  6. Fight. Reel when the fish rests, stop when it runs. Keep tension out of the red; adjust drag with the mouse wheel. Move the mouse sideways to apply side pressure against the fish's direction — it tires faster.
  7. Land it. When the fish is close and tired, press E to net it. Keep it (K) to sell, or release it (R) for bonus points.
  8. Sell & upgrade. Steer to the dock or any bank and press E to go ashore. The Fischerei stall next to the dock buys your catch; spend the francs on faster outboards (each comes with a new hull colour) and braided line.
